# Eurorack Panel Fabrication in Seattle

Future Present Labs fabricates Eurorack panels for modular synth builders who need prototype, first-article, or short-run production hardware. We support flat panel cutting, hole cleanup, engraving, revision marks, and the mechanical details that determine whether a module feels solid once it is mounted in a case.

Eurorack panels look simple until the stackup is wrong. Pot shafts, jack nuts, LED pipes, standoffs, PCB headers, rail clearance, and silkscreen or engraving locations all compete for space. We help review those constraints before cutting so a panel is not just pretty, but usable.

## Eurorack panel work

- 3U and utility module panels from DXF, STEP, SVG, or CAD
- Prototype and short-run aluminum panels
- Jack, pot, switch, LED, and mounting-hole layouts
- Laser engraved labels, revision marks, logos, serial numbers, and QR codes
- Panel blanks that pair with PCB revisions or handwired prototypes

## Fabrication process

Most Eurorack panel jobs start with a flat profile and hole layout. [Waterjet cutting](/seattle-manufacturing/waterjet-cutting-seattle/) is useful for fast panel blanks and odd profiles. [CNC machining](/seattle-manufacturing/cnc-machining-seattle/) can handle critical holes, countersinks, slots, and features that need tighter control. [Laser engraving](/seattle-manufacturing/laser-engraving-seattle/) adds durable labels without relying on stickers or fragile temporary marks.

## What to send

Send panel dimensions, HP width, material, thickness, finish, hole locations, artwork, PCB files if available, and photos or screenshots of the module layout. If the panel must line up with a specific PCB, include the board outline and connector or control locations.
