# Daisy Patch PCB Prototyping

Future Present Labs helps builders prototype Daisy Patch and Daisy-based Eurorack hardware. We support PCB revisions, control layouts, panel fit, carrier boards, test fixtures, and the practical bring-up work required to turn a digital audio idea into a module people can patch.

Daisy Patch projects sit at the intersection of firmware, audio electronics, controls, and mechanical packaging. The PCB has to expose the right knobs, jacks, switches, and debug access while fitting behind a panel and inside a case. Getting that right early saves painful board spins.

## Daisy Patch prototype support

- PCB layout and revision support for Daisy-based modules
- Front-panel control mapping and jack placement
- Board-to-panel alignment review
- Audio I/O, CV, gate, encoder, LED, display, and power considerations
- Prototype bring-up and functional test planning

## From dev board to module

The path from a working patch to a usable module usually needs more than a PCB. We can help with [custom Eurorack panels](/audio-hardware-fabrication/custom-eurorack-panels-waterjet/), [Daisy Seed carrier board design](/audio-hardware-fabrication/daisy-seed-carrier-board-design/), [laser engraved labels](/seattle-manufacturing/laser-engraving-seattle/), and fixture work for repeatable assembly or testing.

## What to send

Send firmware status, schematic or block diagram, desired controls, I/O count, panel width, power requirements, mechanical constraints, and any existing Daisy Patch prototype notes. If the product is headed toward a short run, include target quantity and assembly assumptions.
