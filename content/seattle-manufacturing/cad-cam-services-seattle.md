# CAD/CAM Services in Seattle

Future Present Labs supports Seattle CAD/CAM work for parts that need to become real hardware, not just clean screenshots. We help turn models, drawings, sketches, and revisions into machinable plans for CNC milling, turning, waterjet cutting, engraving, fixtures, and prototypes.

CAD defines the shape. CAM defines how that shape gets made. The gap between them is where many prototype delays happen: no setup face, impossible internal corners, missing tolerances, unclear threads, fragile walls, or geometry that requires a tool the schedule cannot support.

## CAD/CAM support

- CNC programming and machining strategy
- Model cleanup for prototype parts and fixtures
- Drawing review for tolerances, threads, materials, and finishes
- Fixture planning and workholding feedback

## Design for the process

CAD that looks finished may still be difficult to make. Sharp internal corners, deep pockets, unsupported thin walls, missing setup references, and unclear tolerances can all slow a job down. We flag those issues before they become scrap, delay, or surprise cost.

## Useful inputs

Send STEP files, native CAD, drawings, sketches, photos, or an existing part. If the design has to fit another assembly, include the mating geometry or key dimensions so the manufacturing plan reflects the real constraint.

## What better CAD/CAM changes

Good CAD/CAM work can reduce setups, make inspection clearer, choose a better stock shape, split a part into simpler pieces, or add a fixture feature that disappears later. For prototypes, those choices can be the difference between learning this week and waiting for a perfect but impractical design.
