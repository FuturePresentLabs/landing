# PCB Design in Seattle

Future Present Labs helps Seattle hardware teams with PCB design, electronics prototyping, bring-up, and test integration. We are a good fit when your board needs to connect cleanly to mechanical parts, fixtures, sensors, firmware, or manufacturing workflows.

## PCB support

- Schematic capture and PCB layout
- Board revisions for prototypes and small product runs
- Embedded hardware bring-up and bench debugging
- Test fixtures, harnesses, and data logging support

## Integrated mechanical and electronics work

Boards rarely live alone. We can design around enclosures, panel controls, mounting constraints, cable paths, and test access, then support the surrounding [CNC machining](/seattle-manufacturing/cnc-machining-seattle/), [rapid prototyping](/seattle-manufacturing/rapid-prototyping-seattle/), and firmware work.
