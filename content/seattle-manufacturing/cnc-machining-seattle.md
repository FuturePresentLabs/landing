# CNC Machining in Seattle

Future Present Labs is a Seattle machine shop for prototype and short-run CNC parts. We help hardware teams, product designers, defense suppliers, event venues, and local businesses turn CAD files, drawings, or sketches into parts that are ready for test, assembly, or customer use.

## Good fit projects

- Prototype brackets, mounts, plates, adapters, and housings
- Short-run aluminum, stainless, brass, titanium, and plastic parts
- Fixture plates, inspection aids, drill guides, and assembly tooling
- Iterative parts that need quick feedback from the person making them

## Why work locally

Local CNC machining makes iteration faster. You can review photos, talk through tolerances, update CAD, and keep the next revision moving without shipping delays or long quoting queues.
