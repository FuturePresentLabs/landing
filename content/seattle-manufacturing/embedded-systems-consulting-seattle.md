# Embedded Systems Consulting in Seattle

Future Present Labs provides embedded systems consulting in Seattle for teams building products, fixtures, sensors, controls, and test hardware. We help when firmware needs to connect to real hardware, and when the physical setup needs to support reliable testing.

Embedded projects become expensive when the team cannot see what the system is doing. Good logs, repeatable fixtures, known-good hardware, and simple test scripts can save more time than a large rewrite.

## Embedded support

- Firmware patches, debugging, and bring-up support
- Sensor integration, controls, and data capture
- Test scripts, logging tools, and production aids
- Hardware review around connectors, power, mounting, and service access

## Practical firmware for physical products

Embedded work often fails at the boundary between code and hardware. We can help build or revise the fixture, panel, harness, or machined part that makes firmware testing repeatable. That keeps debugging grounded in the same physical system the product will use.

## When to involve us

Bring us in when a board needs initial bring-up, a sensor needs validation, a test rig needs automation, or a product needs a small firmware change to get through the next prototype milestone.

## Firmware tied to physical reality

We keep embedded work connected to the bench. That can mean a machined sensor mount, a printed fixture, a harness, a temporary panel, a data capture script, or a small fixture board. The goal is to make the next failure visible and repeatable enough to fix.
