# Engineering Prototyping in Seattle

Engineering prototypes need to answer a technical question. Will the part fit? Will the fixture hold repeatably? Can the board survive the test? Does the firmware capture the right data? Future Present Labs helps Seattle teams build prototypes around those questions.

We are most useful when the prototype crosses domains. A machined part may need a board mount. A PCB may need a fixture. A firmware test may need a repeatable sensor position. A customer demo may need enough polish to be trusted without pretending it is final production.

## Prototype support

- Machined and printed parts for fit, strength, and assembly testing
- PCB design support, bring-up, and embedded debugging
- Test fixtures, harnesses, and data logging tools
- CAD/CAM feedback before committing to a more expensive revision

## Built around the next decision

A good engineering prototype does not need to look like a finished product unless appearance is part of the test. We help identify the fastest build that produces useful evidence, then support the next revision with what was learned.

## Mechanical plus electronics

Many prototypes fail at the interface between domains: mounting holes, wire exits, heat, service access, fasteners, or test points. Because we work across machining, PCB support, firmware, and fixtures, we can catch those issues earlier.

## What a good prototype plan includes

A useful plan names the test, not just the object. Tell us the pass/fail criteria, what environment the prototype sees, what parts are fixed, and what can change. That lets us separate must-have precision from details that can wait until the next revision.
