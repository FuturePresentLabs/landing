# Product Prototyping in Seattle

Future Present Labs helps Seattle teams turn product concepts into functional prototypes. We are a good fit for physical products that involve machined parts, printed fixtures, embedded electronics, panels, housings, or test hardware.

Product prototypes have different jobs at different stages. A proof-of-concept build can be ugly if it proves the core mechanism. A pilot unit needs better fit, labeling, assembly sequence, and service access. A customer demo needs the details that make people believe the product is real.

## Prototype stages we support

- Proof-of-concept hardware to test feasibility
- Functional prototypes for fit, assembly, and real-world use
- Investor, demo, or pilot units that need clean marking and finish details
- Revision support after testing reveals what needs to change

## What to bring

Useful starting points include CAD, sketches, photos, block diagrams, existing samples, or a short description of the user problem. If you already know the target quantity, material constraints, cost target, or use environment, include those early.

## Practical product development

We can help with [CNC machining](/seattle-manufacturing/cnc-machining-seattle/), [3D printing](/seattle-manufacturing/3d-printing-seattle/), [PCB design](/seattle-manufacturing/pcb-design-seattle/), [laser engraving](/seattle-manufacturing/laser-engraving-seattle/), and fixture work. When a task belongs with a specialist partner, we will keep that boundary clear.

## Avoiding expensive prototype mistakes

Common traps include designing around unavailable components, hiding fasteners until assembly is painful, leaving no room for wires, making every surface cosmetic, and choosing a manufacturing process before the design is stable. We help identify those issues early so the next build teaches you something useful.
