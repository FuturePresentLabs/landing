# Rapid Prototyping in Seattle

Future Present Labs helps Seattle teams move from idea to working prototype with fewer handoffs. We combine CNC machining, 3D printing, electronics, engraving, waterjet cutting, and practical design support for projects where the next useful revision matters more than a polished process deck.

## Prototype work we handle

- First-article machined parts and fixture plates
- Functional 3D printed tooling and assembly aids
- Electronics prototypes, harnesses, and test rigs
- Marked panels, labels, and short-run presentation parts

## Faster iteration

Local prototyping keeps feedback loops short. We can talk through CAD, identify risky features, suggest materials, and help decide what should be machined, printed, engraved, or cut first.
