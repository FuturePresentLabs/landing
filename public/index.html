Core capabilities

Our Seattle manufacturing services combine machining, cutting, marking, electronics, and prototype support in one local workflow. Common entry points include CNC machining, waterjet cutting, and laser engraving for teams that need parts made, marked, and ready for assembly.

We also support audio hardware fabrication for boutique synth and pedal builders, including Eurorack panels and cases, guitar pedal enclosure machining, Daisy Seed carrier boards, and Daisy Patch PCB prototypes.

CNC machining

4-axis milling in aluminum, stainless, brass, titanium, and engineering plastics. Setups stay ready for quick reruns.

  • Prototyping and production runs
  • Custom Fixturing
  • Surface finishing through local partners

CNC turning

Lathe Turning for bushings, shafts, and housings with hand-fit checks before shipping.

  • Brass, Aluminum, Delrin & More
  • Threading, grooving, and parting tools on-hand
  • From 5 parts to 500+

3D printing

Carbon-filled nylon and engineering-grade plastics for tooling, fixtures, and workholding.

  • Printer-ready in hours for fixtures or quick parts
  • Hybrid Carbon Nylon prints + machined plates for rigid tooling
  • Bulk PA6-CF Parts

Waterjet cutting

WAZER abrasive waterjet cutting for metals, plastics, composites, rubber, and gasket materials without heat-affected edges.

  • DXF, STEP, and sketch-to-cut support
  • Great for brackets, panels, shims, and blanks
  • Machined finishing available after cutting

Laser engraving

MOPA fiber laser engraving for permanent part IDs, serial numbers, branding, panels, and short-run product details.

  • Aluminum, stainless, plastics, coated parts, and wood
  • Batch serialization and layout support
  • Engraving paired with machining or assembly

PCB design

Circuit board design, bring-up, and test support for embedded products, fixtures, and hardware prototypes.

  • Schematic capture and board layout
  • Prototype assembly and bench debugging
  • Firmware and test integration

Software Development

Lightweight firmware, scripts, and automation to get business problems solved fast.

  • Inventory management + e-commerce
  • Data logging or control scripts for test rigs
  • Apps to link tooling, machining, and reporting
