Small Batch Manufacturing in Seattle

Future Present Labs helps Seattle teams with small-batch manufacturing when the job is too real for a one-off prototype but not ready for high-volume production. This is the space where repeatability, setup notes, packaging, and revision control start to matter.

Small batches are often where a product learns how it wants to be made. The first five parts prove the design; the next 25 expose what takes too long, what needs a fixture, what should be engraved, and what needs clearer inspection criteria.

Small-batch work we support

  • Repeat machined parts in quantities from a few pieces to short production runs
  • Engraved and serialized components
  • Fixture parts, tooling, and assembly aids
  • Prototype assemblies that need consistent packaging and handoff notes

Why local helps

Small-batch runs often reveal small issues: a hole pattern that needs adjustment, a finish that should change, a packaging requirement, or a tolerance that only matters in one spot. Working locally keeps those discoveries from becoming multi-week delays.

From prototype to repeat run

If we made the prototype, we can preserve the useful setup knowledge for the next batch. If another shop made the first revision, send the CAD, drawings, photos, and any known fit issues so we can evaluate the repeat run cleanly.

What changes after the first article

Once the first article is approved, the work shifts toward consistency: stable material, repeatable fixturing, controlled revisions, labeled bags or kits, and agreed inspection points. We can also mark parts with serial numbers or revision IDs so field feedback maps back to the right batch.
