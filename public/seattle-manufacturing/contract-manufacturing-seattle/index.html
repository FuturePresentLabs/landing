Contract Manufacturing in Seattle

Future Present Labs supports contract manufacturing in Seattle for teams that need a practical local partner for parts, fixtures, small builds, and engineering-heavy production support. We are not a large factory; we are a small technical shop that is useful when the work still needs attention, context, and iteration.

That distinction matters. If you need thousands of mature parts per month, we may help with prototyping, fixtures, or supplier handoff. If you need low-volume parts, debug-heavy assemblies, or a manufacturing partner who can still talk through the design, we are a better fit.

Best fit contract work

  • Low-volume machined components and repeat prototype parts
  • Fixture kits, assembly aids, and test hardware
  • Engraved, serialized, or labeled parts
  • Mechanical work that connects to PCB, firmware, or embedded systems projects

Where we add value

The highest-value contract work for us is usually mixed-domain: a machined enclosure with a PCB, a test fixture with firmware, a production aid that needs data logging, or a product component that needs both mechanical and electrical review.

Clear scope matters

For contract manufacturing, send revision-controlled files, quantities, expected cadence, quality requirements, and packaging needs. If the product is still changing, we can help separate prototype work from repeatable production work so the plan stays realistic.

How we keep the work controlled

Good contract work needs revision discipline. We track what file set was quoted, what revision shipped, what inspection points matter, and what changes before the next batch. For mixed hardware, we can also support fixtures, firmware scripts, or data logging so testing is not left as an afterthought.
